About me

Thank you for checking out my portfolio! My name is Loek van Kessel. I am 20 years old and studying game development at St.Lucas Eindhoven!

I find making games to be very interesting and enjoyable, but after a few years I've discovered I'm even more interested in game design! It's a game designer's job to make games fun and satisfying to play, and the psychology, techniques and tricks behind it are endlessly fascinating to me which is why I'm trying to be accepted into BUas! (Breda University of Applied Sciences)

Other than my studies I enjoy to do some gaming of my own, and managing my own little homelab where I experiment using virtual machines and run services to make my life a little easier.
